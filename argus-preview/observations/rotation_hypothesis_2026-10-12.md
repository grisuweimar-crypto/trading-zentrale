# ARGUS prospective research hypothesis: AI concentration / sector rotation

- Registered: 2026-10-12 (Europe/Berlin)
- Status: PROPOSED / RESEARCH_ONLY
- Production impact: NONE
- Origin: user hypothesis, registered before prospective outcome measurement
- Hypothesis ID: ROTATION_AI_2026_01

## Thesis
If AI/hyperscaler market leadership weakens, selected reasonably valued, financially robust and economically less AI-capex-dependent equities may outperform both the AI-heavy leaders and the user's passive ETF benchmark. This may occur through relative outperformance even without absolute gains or an AI crash.

## Competing scenarios
- H1: Sector rotation with positive absolute returns for selected non-AI equities.
- H2: Relative outperformance without positive absolute returns during a broad drawdown.
- H3: Continued AI leadership; rotation thesis fails.
- H4: Broad risk-off drawdown, with limited investable separation.

## Ex-ante observations to preserve
Record point-in-time universe membership, original-currency prices, available fundamentals and their publication dates, valuation, quality, earnings revisions, 3/6/12-month relative strength, AI-capex economic exposure, sector, market beta, transaction costs, ETF comparator, and decision timestamp. Do not backfill current knowledge into past snapshots.

## Candidate selection
Selection must use frozen, predeclared rules and data available at the decision timestamp. No retrospective winners lists. A candidate is not a buy signal. Keep discovery, validation, and untouched holdout separate. Use a cooldown for overlapping observations and cluster-aware uncertainty.

## Primary evaluation
Compare prospective total return after costs and applicable tax assumptions against the actual user ETF over matching holding windows and cashflows; also compare against buy-and-hold and sector/equal-weight alternatives. Measure risk, maximum drawdown, turnover, hit rate, calibration, and net incremental value. Distinguish excess return from merely reduced drawdown. Report both absolute and relative outcomes.
