## Hard review rules

A reviewer may approve a candidate only after checking that the cited document supports both the subject/factor relationship and the proposed relationship class. Approval creates no bullish/bearish sign, no weight and no threshold. The approved mapping becomes valid no earlier than the review timestamp. Rejecting a candidate does not remove the subject from the 207-stock research domain; it remains visible and must later be mapped differently or explicitly marked unmapped.
