# QM-C3 — Multiplicity & Sequential Monitoring

QM-C3 extends the same governance chain established by QM-C1 and QM-C2. It does not introduce a separate statistical namespace. A control-plan member is an exact versioned tuple of the QM-C1 hypothesis, QM-C2 analysis plan and QM-A analysis version.

## Purpose

QM-C3 prevents two common forms of post-hoc result shopping:

1. trying several related confirmatory hypotheses and reporting only a favourable member without a predeclared family/error-control rule;
2. repeatedly inspecting outcomes until a favourable result appears without a predeclared monitoring schedule.

QM-C3 governs these choices before confirmatory outcome inspection. It does not calculate effects or adjusted p-values.

## Exact family membership

Every family member stores and validates:

- `hypothesis_id`
- `hypothesis_version`
- `hypothesis_version_hash`
- `analysis_plan_id`
- `analysis_plan_version`
- `analysis_plan_hash`
- `qm_a_analysis_id`
- `qm_a_version_id`

All members must share the exact QM-C1 `hypothesis_family_id`. Duplicate hypothesis versions or duplicate analysis-plan versions are rejected.

At control-plan freeze, every member must pass the full QM-C2 confirmation-readiness gate. This means QM-C1, QM-C2, QM-A and QM-B have already agreed on the exact frozen identity, and QM-B strict-universe blockers cannot be bypassed by QM-C3.

## Multiplicity strategies

The control plan requires one predeclared strategy:

- `PREDECLARED_SINGLE_PRIMARY` — exactly one family member; no multiplicity parameters.
- `BONFERRONI_FWER` — requires a predeclared `family_alpha` in `(0,1)`.
- `HOLM_FWER` — requires a predeclared `family_alpha` in `(0,1)`.
- `BENJAMINI_HOCHBERG_FDR` — requires a predeclared `fdr_q` in `(0,1)`.
- `CUSTOM_PREDECLARED` — requires a method name, immutable rule hash and rationale.

QM-C3 records the rule. It deliberately does not implement generic adjusted-p-value calculations because not every research endpoint is a p-value problem and analysis-specific statistical execution belongs to the governed evaluation, not to the registry.

## Sequential monitoring

Every control plan also freezes a monitoring schedule. Supported contract forms are:

- `FIXED_HORIZON_NO_INTERIM`
- `PREDECLARED_LOOKS`
- `CUSTOM_PREDECLARED`

Every planned look has a stable `look_id` and an `information_fraction`. Fractions must be strictly increasing and the final look must be `1.0`. Fixed-horizon evaluation has exactly one final look and cannot permit early stopping.

The monitoring registry enforces the sequence mechanically. A later look cannot be recorded before an earlier one, an undeclared look cannot be inserted, and no look can be added after `COMPLETE` or `STOPPED`.

## QM-A evidence-state connection

The first recorded outcome look requires every family member's QM-A analysis to still be `FROZEN_FOR_CONFIRMATION`. This proves that the first outcome inspection did not begin from an already consumed confirmatory state.

If another predeclared look follows, QM-C3 requires QM-A to show a consumed/evaluated confirmatory state before that later look is accepted. In practice the first look is therefore followed by the normal QM-A transition to `CONFIRMATORY_EVALUATED` (or a later spent state) before any second look can occur.

This prevents a sequence of repeated looks from remaining falsely labelled as untouched confirmatory evidence.

## Early stopping

Interim decisions are one of:

- `CONTINUE`
- `STOP_EFFICACY`
- `STOP_FUTILITY`

Early-stop decisions are accepted only when `early_stop_allowed` was frozen in advance. The final scheduled look must use `FINAL_COMPLETE`. Once a family is stopped or completed, further looks fail closed.

## Versioning and integrity

Control plans are append-only and hash-chained. The semantic plan receives an immutable `control_plan_hash`. A change to family membership, multiplicity strategy, parameters, look schedule or stopping rule requires an explicit successor `control_plan_version`; an existing version cannot be rewritten.

Monitoring-look history is part of the same hash chain and therefore cannot be silently deleted or reordered without failing integrity verification.

## Canonical confirmatory path after QM-C3

The governed path is now:

1. QM-C1: register exact confirmatory hypothesis version.
2. QM-C2: register and freeze its analysis plan before hypothesis freeze.
3. QM-A: freeze the exact immutable analysis identity generated by QM-C2.
4. QM-C1: freeze the hypothesis version.
5. QM-C3: register and freeze the exact hypothesis-family/control-plan membership and monitoring rules.
6. QM-C3: validate `evaluation_ready` before the first outcome look.
7. Record only predeclared looks; update QM-A evidence state after evidence is consumed.

A C1 or C2 frozen object alone is therefore not the final pre-evaluation gate once QM-C3 is active.

## Boundaries

QM-C3 does not change scanner scores, Selection, Timing, Probability, Risk, Confidence, Elliott, Decision Layer, portfolio actions or execution. It does not compute effects or adjusted p-values and does not claim empirical promotion.

Negative/rejected-result retention, duplicate-hypothesis audit and full QM-C/BA-QM2 closure remain the single next functional block: QM-C4.
